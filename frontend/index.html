WHAT HAPPENED

Heavy rain triggered widespread landslides, damaged roads and homes, and caused major loss of life across the Nilgiris.

IF AXON HAD BEEN DEPLOYED

Ground precursors + rainfall context → local model risk → unsafe-corridor rejection → safe-route guidance → LoRa community warning, including during connectivity failure.

IMPACT ESTIMATE

Historical fatalities: 44. These are people who were exposed in the real event, not a scientifically proven number AXON would have saved. The defensible claim is that AXON could have provided earlier warning and reduced exposure for at-risk communities.
